Project Overview

Arizona State University | NIRSense Research Lab | Graduate Research Assistant | 2026

The NIRSense Human-Autonomy Teaming Study is a human factors research project examining human performance and participant experience in a controlled human-autonomy teaming environment.

Human-autonomy teaming involves people working alongside automated or autonomous systems to complete tasks. Understanding how people experience these interactions is important for designing systems that support effective coordination and human performance.

As a Graduate Research Assistant, I support the research team in preparing study materials, coordinating participant sessions, administering research instruments, and maintaining study documentation.

My Role: Graduate Research Assistant supporting human-subjects research activities, participant sessions, data collection, and research documentation.

Research Context

As autonomous systems become more common, it is important to understand how they influence human performance and teamwork.

Human-autonomy teaming research considers how people coordinate activities, manage workload, interpret information, and respond while interacting with automated systems.

The NIRSense study provides a controlled research environment for investigating aspects of human performance and participant experience.

Research Focus:

Understanding human performance, workload, coordination, and participant experience in human-autonomy teaming.
